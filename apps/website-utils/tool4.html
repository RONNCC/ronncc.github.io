MMS Image Resizer

Paste an image and get a version that will actually send via Google Voice / MMS.

Per Google's docs, images over 2 MB get auto-compressed into a smaller file and US carriers commonly cap MMS at ~1–1.2 MB, so this tool caps the long edge at 1600 px and re-encodes as JPEG to squeeze under your chosen target. The whole thing runs locally in your browser.

Paste your image here with ⌘/Ctrl + V, drag & drop it, or click to browse
