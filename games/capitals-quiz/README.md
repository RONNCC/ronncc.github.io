# Capitals of the World

Learn the capital of every country in the world and every US state, one module at a time.

## How it works

1. **Choose your modules** — World (all ~195 countries), any individual continent (North America, South America, Europe, Asia, Africa, Oceania), or US States. You can pick several at once.
2. **Choose a question count** — 10, 20, 50, or all available entries.
3. **Start the quiz** — you're shown a country (or state) and type its capital. Answers are accent- and punctuation-insensitive, and a few special cases accept multiple correct answers (e.g. Bolivia: Sucre or La Paz; Sri Lanka: Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte or Colombo; Tanzania: Dodoma or Dar es Salaam).
4. **Track your progress** — score, streaks, and best score per module + question-count combo are saved in `localStorage`. After a run you can review only the entries you missed.

There is also a **Study / Review** mode with searchable country → capital tables and flashcards for each selected module.

## Files

- `index.html` — the entire app (UI, CSS, data, and quiz logic) in one file.

## Data

- All 195 sovereign-country capitals grouped by continent, plus all 50 US state capitals, embedded in the page — no network requests needed at runtime.

## Local usage

Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything (including scores) stays on your machine.
